Rules & Guidelines

Please read the competition rules carefully before participating.

General Conduct
  • Do not attack the infrastructure hosting the CTF platform.
  • No brute-forcing challenge flags or login endpoints.
  • Do not share flags or solutions with other teams during the event.
  • Collaboration within your registered team (max 4 members) is encouraged.
Flag Format

All flags follow the standard Nexus CTF format unless explicitly specified in the challenge description:

NEXUS{this_is_an_example_flag_12345}

Flags are case-sensitive. Ensure you submit the complete string including the curly braces.

Scoring System

Challenges use a dynamic or fixed point system ranging from 100 to 500 points based on difficulty. In the event of a tie in total score, the team that achieved their final score first will be ranked higher.

Support & Help

If you encounter broken challenge instances or have urgent inquiries, reach out to the admin crew on our official Discord server or open an admin ticket in your dashboard.
